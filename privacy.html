Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Blork Studios ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit this website.

1. Information Collected

This website is an informational studio website. It does not currently require user accounts, a newsletter signup, or a contact form, and it does not intentionally collect personal visitor information. If you send us an email directly, we receive the information you choose to include in your correspondence.

The hosting provider and network infrastructure delivering this static site may record standard technical server logs (such as IP address, browser user-agent, device details, and request timestamps) for security, caching, and routing purposes.

2. Cookies & Analytics

Blork Studios does not set advertising cookies or employ invasive third-party tracking networks on this website. If analytics or embedded media services are introduced in the future, this policy will be promptly updated.

3. External Links & Social Platforms

This website provides links to external channels including X/Twitter, Facebook, LinkedIn, and email protocols. These services operate independently under their own terms and privacy practices.

4. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for general audiences interested in video games and is not directed at children under 13.

5. Security

We maintain strict static hosting standards and secure protocols (HTTPS). No electronic transmission over the public internet can be guaranteed 100% impenetrable.

6. Contact & Inquiries

If you have any questions or privacy inquiries, contact us directly at blorkstudios@gmail.com.
