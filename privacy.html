PLAIN ENGLISH TL;DR // THE SHORT VERSION

Blork Studios is an independent solo game studio. We make games like Art of War: Setting Sun, not ad-surveillance software. We do not sell your personal information, we do not run third-party advertising tracking networks, and we don't harvest your data. Below is the complete legal explanation of what little technical data is processed when you visit our website or follow our devlogs.

01 // Scope & Operator

Who Operates This Service

This website (including all subpages, assets, and development dispatches) is operated directly by Philip Gonzales, doing business as Blork Studios ("we," "us," or "our").

This policy applies to your use of this studio website, our official game development updates, and correspondence transmitted directly to our studio communication channels.

02 // Data Collection & Server Infrastructure

What Information Is (and Is Not) Collected

We deliberately minimize data collection across our entire web presence:

  • No User Accounts: This website does not require account creation, password databases, or user profiles.
  • No Marketing Beacons: We do not embed Facebook Pixels, TikTok trackers, or behavioral ad scripts.
  • Standard Server Logs: The infrastructure hosting this static site (such as GitHub Pages or secure CDNs) automatically logs basic technical routing metadata (IP addresses, browser user-agents, referring pages, and HTTP status codes) strictly for security diagnostics, caching, and defending against DDoS attacks.
  • Direct Correspondence: If you voluntarily transmit an email to blorkstudios@gmail.com, we receive your email address, name (if attached), and the message contents to respond to your inquiry.
03 // Cookies & Local Storage

Our Stance on Cookies

Blork Studios does not set cross-site tracking cookies, behavioral profiling cookies, or third-party marketing beacons.

Any client-side browser storage utilized on this site (such as modern localStorage) is strictly functional—used only to preserve client preferences (such as audio volume or interface state) locally on your own machine.

04 // External Game Platforms & Stores

Third-Party Platform Links

To follow development logs or wishlist upcoming releases, this site links to official external platforms:

  • Itch.io: Our active development log is hosted on Itch.io (blork-studios.itch.io). Interacting with the Itch platform is governed by Itch.io's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Valve Steam: Future desktop release listings and wishlists operate under Valve Corporation's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Store: Future Android mobile builds operate under Google LLC's Privacy Policy.
  • Community & Social: Links to our official X/Twitter, Facebook, and LinkedIn profiles operate under their respective privacy policies.
05 // Future Game Test Builds & Alpha Telemetry

Playtests & Crash Diagnostics

As Art of War: Setting Sun advances through public playtests, playable builds may include basic, automated crash reporting.

This telemetry is strictly limited to non-identifying technical data (e.g., graphics driver version, operating system, crash stack trace, and game version) used solely to diagnose engine crashes, fix bugs, and optimize performance. We never access, scan, or transmit private personal files from your computer or mobile device.

06 // Children's Privacy

Younger Players

Our website and games are intended for general video gaming audiences. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal contact information, please notify us immediately so we can expunge it.

07 // Your Rights & Data Deletion

Player Rights & Control

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR in Europe and CCPA in California), you have rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to request confirmation of whether we hold any personal information regarding you.
  • The right to request the prompt deletion of any past email correspondence or tester records.
  • The right to withdraw any consent previously provided for direct communications.

Questions Regarding Your Privacy?

Communications are answered personally by solo developer Philip Gonzales.
