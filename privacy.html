Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Blork Studios ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit this website.

1. Information this website collects

This website is an informational studio website. It does not currently provide user accounts, a newsletter signup, or a contact form, and it does not intentionally ask visitors to submit personal information.

The hosting provider and infrastructure used to deliver the website may automatically process technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, requested pages, and request timestamps in server or security logs. We do not operate those hosting logs directly. Please consult the hosting provider's privacy documentation for details about its processing and retention.

2. Cookies, analytics, and tracking

Blork Studios does not intentionally set advertising cookies or operate third-party analytics on this website at the time of publication. If analytics, embedded media, advertising, or other tracking services are added, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant technologies and choices.

3. Links to other services

This website links to third-party services, including Portal Music and GitHub. Those services operate independently and have their own privacy policies and practices. Your activity on an external service is governed by that provider's terms and privacy policy.

Portal Music is a separate service and may offer account sign-in, saved favorites, playlists, or other features. Its own Privacy Policy explains how it handles information within that service.

4. Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through an external communication channel, contact us using the public studio profile linked below so we can review the concern.

5. Security

We use a static website and take reasonable care when maintaining its content and configuration. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Privacy rights and requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information processed about you. Because this website does not provide accounts or collect information through a form, most requests will relate to technical hosting logs or a third-party service. Contact the relevant provider about information it controls.

7. Changes

We may update this policy as the website or our practices change. The date above indicates the latest revision.

8. Contact

For privacy questions about this studio website, contact Blork Studios through its public GitHub profile. Please do not post sensitive personal information publicly.

Important: This is a practical starting point for the current static website, not legal advice. It must be updated if you add forms, mailing lists, analytics, advertising, accounts, or other data collection. The final policy should accurately describe the hosting and services actually in use.
