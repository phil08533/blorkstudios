Think RuneScape's progression, Skyrim's wandering and Destiny's gunplay, rendered like a lost PS1 disc. Every shot below is straight out of the current build.

01 / FIGHT

Read the red. Or eat it.

Enemies telegraph everything. Gunners paint a lane before the blast, brawlers light up the ground before the slam, and beast packs circle while one pounces. Dodge, punish, and loot a weapon ladder that runs from Lvl 1 blaster to Lvl 100 heavy.

When on foot isn't enough, pull a Battle Car, Tank or Mech out of Fort Ironrock and hold the trigger.

Blasters to laser swords Wizard staffs Battle mechs Boss intros
02 / FLY

Your ship is your house.

The Setting Sun is a battered transport you live in. Walk to the helm, take off through the atmosphere, flip to cockpit view, scan the star charts and set her down on a landing pad on the next world.

Dogfight raiders, dock with pirate ships and clear them room by room, then go home and add a gun range, a fish tank or a very illegal lab.

Takeoff & landing Cockpit view Ship boarding Rooms you build
03 / ARENA PRIME

Thousands watching. No pressure.

Grab a snack at the food stalls, walk out of the tunnel and fight wave after wave while the stadium gets louder. Bet on bouts, take on bosses, or go head to head with other players.

PvE waves PvP Betting
04 / CHOOSE A SIDE

The badge, or the black market.

Marshals work for Marshal Rook: bounties, busts, holding cells and an evidence locker full of other people's stash.

Outlaws build a lab on the ship, cook their own product, hire crew, run street dealers and keep one eye on their heat. You can't do both. Choose carefully.

THE STUDIO

BLORK STUDIOS.
WE MAKE WEIRD WORLDS.

Blork Studios is a one-person indie studio run by Philip Gonzales. Art of War started in high school, drawn by Phil and his buddy Caleb in the margins of class notebooks: the logo, the Masters, the guns, the bugs, the planets. Blork is turning every page of it into a real game.

No checklists and no corporate templates. Just big, strange sci-fi with heart, humour and a little bit of random b******t.
