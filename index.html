02 / FLAGSHIP TITLE

ART OF WAR:
SETTING SUN

A sci-fi first-person shooter MMORPG in one shared galaxy. Think RuneScape's progression + Skyrim's exploration + Destiny's gunplay & loot, wrapped in a stylized retro PS1 aesthetic.

RETRO PS1 FPS MMO • LIVE BUILDS

YOUR SHIP IS YOUR HOME BASE

THE SETTING SUN AWAITS.

You start in apartment 1C on Earth, transplant a snarky robot head named B.U.R.P. into your brain, and take the helm of a battered transport called the Setting Sun. Expand your ship room by room — bridge, quarters, armory, drug lab, trophy hall, and vehicle bay — as you launch between 17+ planets with takeoff sequence transitions into open space.

B.U.R.P. Neural AI: "Testing sound transplant. Yup I can hear your thoughts and see what you see... remember that when you use the toilet please."
FPS + Spaceflight 17+ Landable Worlds Deep Alien Fishing 4 Legendary Masters Vehicle & Mech Combat Shared Online Galaxy

02 / CORE GAMEPLAY PILLARS

BUILT FOR
EXPLORATION & COMBAT.

From tactical FPS firefights and deep-space dogfights to alien pond fishing and underground poker dens.

Pillar 01

FPS Gunplay & Loot

Full 1-100 tier weapon ladder (blasters, SMGs, heavy rifles, laser swords, wizard staffs) with gun mods, elemental ammo, and signature Master gear.

Pillar 02

Spaceflight & Takeoff

Board your cockpit, launch through the planetary takeoff sequence loading screen, scan orbital star charts, dogfight raiders, and board drifting derelict freighters.

Pillar 03

Deep Life & Economy

Safe hubs with clinics, banks, and pawn shops. Catch 10+ species of alien fish, race hover cars on Velocity, or craft substances in your ship's illicit lab.

Pillar 04

The Warlord Drome

Battle Drome's robot legions (XR-17s, hover-bots, riot droids) across rotating world raids, ending in an epic cosmic finale at the Greater Maw.

04 / THE GALAXY MAP

17 WORLDS.
ONE SHARED UNIVERSE.

Each planet offers its own distinct biomes, factions, storylines, radio stations, and secrets.

01 / HUB WORLD

Earth & New Jamestown

Walled capital with Town Hall, the Roxy Cinema, Cookie's Diner, Jamestown Arms apartments, and Captain Vega's militia.

02 / RINGED WORLD

Mezar

Honey-amber desert world housing Bug City beehive towers, the Sand Dome Desert PvP zone, and the Bug Master.

03 / DEEP TEAL FOREST

Capitone

Frozen pines, the Mountain of the Lost, Volcano Forge dungeon, and the Cyclops knight Master Maxurge.

04 / NEON WASTES

Robot City

Tower of Death, robot graveyards, hover-car races, and Ada-9's underground synth resistance.

05 / FLYING METROPOLIS

The Zeltac Ship

A massive 20-floor city ship: Plaza shopping mall, Starlight Lounge romance hub, and Brakka's shipyard.

06 / UNDERWATER DOMES

Ocean World

Port Kelp piers, kayak rental, the Leviathan deep trench, and Bubble City's submerged biodomes.

07 / RACING PLANET

Velocity

Magnet storms and rainbow lightning. 8 hover-car chassis sprinting through canyon point-to-point circuits.

08 / THE EYE IN THE DARK

Vector & The Greater Maw

Past the edge of the star charts, where cosmic anomalies distort reality and the final battle unfolds.
