WHY BLORK?

MAKE SOMETHING
WORTH REMEMBERING.

Blork Studios was born from a straightforward observation: too many games look, play, and sound like they were assembled by committee.

We believe that interactive entertainment shines brightest when it carries genuine creative risk. When a planet doesn't look like generic beige rocks, when the local bartender has an existential crisis about quantum physics, and when your ship feels like a beloved rustbucket held together by spit and optimism.

Our current focus is Art of War: Setting Sun, a massive passion project weaving exploration, eccentric NPC dialogues, tactical encounters, and rich environmental storytelling.

OUR CORE PRINCIPLES

HOW WE
BUILD.

Four bedrock pillars that guide every design decision, line of dialogue, and art asset we craft.

Value 01

Distinctive Soul

Every asset, character, and level has intention. If it feels generic, we scrap it and start over until it has grit and flavor.

Value 02

Player Respect

No predatory monetization, no hollow time sinks, and no handholding. We trust players to think, explore, and experiment.

Value 03

Living Worlds

Ecosystems, stations, and cities that have their own rhythm, internal logic, and quirky social hierarchies.

Value 04

Pure Independence

Being indie allows us to take audacious swings and build the kinds of games we genuinely love playing ourselves.
