WHY BLORK?

Make something
worth remembering.

Blork Studios is an independent game studio built around one simple idea: games should have character. The worlds should surprise you. The people in them should stick with you. And the details should feel like somebody cared enough to make them weird in just the right way.

Our first project is Art of War: Rising Sun, a sci-fi adventure that mixes exploration, comedy, horror, and the occasional unexpectedly human moment.
