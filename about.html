ABOUT THE STUDIO

Small studio.
Unusual ideas.

Blork Studios is an independent creative studio focused on original games and practical tools for people who make things.

WHY BLORK?

Make things that
feel like themselves.

The best ideas don't always fit neatly into a category. Blork Studios is a home for projects with their own personality: strange worlds, memorable characters, and useful creative resources.

That means building games with room for discovery and unexpected moments, alongside tools that remove friction for other creators. The projects may look different, but they share the same goal: make something thoughtful, distinctive, and fun to use.

Blork Studios is currently focused on Art of War: Rising Sun and Portal Music.
