Art of War: Rising Sun is a strange, sprawling sci-fi adventure about exploring a universe that refuses to behave normally.

Travel between distinctive worlds, meet memorable characters, take on unexpected jobs, and stumble into stories that can be hilarious, unsettling, or surprisingly heartfelt. At the center is the Setting Sun: a ship, a crew, and a growing collection of stories waiting to happen.
