01 / THE EXPERIENCE

OUT HERE,
ANYTHING GOES.

RuneScape's world progression + Skyrim's planetary exploration + Destiny's gunplay & loot in an authentic retro PS1 presentation.

Pillar 01

17+ Shared Worlds

From the walled capital of New Jamestown to the neon canyons of Velocity and the underwater biodomes of Ocean World.

Pillar 02

The Setting Sun

Your living starship and mobile base. Launch into deep space through atmospheric takeoff sequences (loading screen transition), decorate interiors, run an illicit chemistry lab, and sleep for the Well Rested buff.

Pillar 03

Legendary Masters

Train under Elismas (Guns & Jetpack), Ramick (Swords), Maxurge (Staffs), and the Bug Master for signature rewards and capes.

Pillar 04

Tactical Variety

First-person gunplay, wizard spells, speed bike racing, drivable battle tanks, mechs, and alien pond fishing.
