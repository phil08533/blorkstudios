THE ADVENTURE

Space is big.
People are weird.

Art of War: Rising Sun is an independent sci-fi adventure built around exploration, strange encounters, and the stories that happen between the stars.

Travel between distinctive worlds, meet memorable characters, take on unexpected jobs, and make your way through a universe that can be funny, unsettling, and surprisingly human. At the center is the Setting Sun: a ship, a crew, and a growing collection of stories waiting to happen.
